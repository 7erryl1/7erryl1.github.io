隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 9 日

本政策說明 Ferrokin iOS App 如何處理資料。Ferrokin 不需要登入帳號。我們不透過 App 收集或接收你的個人資料；音訊分析、外觀設定與寵物狀態均在你的裝置上處理。

麥克風

只有在你按下「麥克風」並允許存取後，App 才會使用麥克風。聲音只在你的裝置上即時分析音量與節奏，用來讓畫面隨音樂律動。App 不會錄音、不會儲存麥克風音訊，也不會將音訊上傳或分享。

你可以隨時在 iPhone 的「設定 › 隱私權與安全性 › 麥克風」關閉 Ferrokin 的麥克風權限。

匯入的音檔

你選擇匯入的音檔只在裝置上播放與分析，不會由 Ferrokin 上傳。

設定與寵物狀態

外觀、燈光、儲存的組合與寵物體力等設定只保存在你的裝置上，不會傳送給我們。刪除 App 時，App 在裝置上保存的本機資料也會移除。

App 內購買

Ferrokin PRO 的付款由 Apple App Store 處理。我們不取得你的信用卡或其他付款方式資料。Apple 如何處理購買相關資料，請參閱 Apple 隱私權政策：https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/。

廣告、分析與追蹤

Ferrokin 沒有廣告、沒有使用者分析工具、沒有跨 App 或網站追蹤，也不使用第三方 SDK 收集個人資料。

兒童隱私

我們不透過 Ferrokin App 收集任何使用者的個人資料，包括兒童的個人資料。

支援聯絡

若你主動透過 Email 聯絡我們，我們會收到你的 Email 地址及你提供的內容，並僅用於回覆與處理你的詢問。請避免寄送密碼、付款資料或其他不必要的敏感資訊。

聯絡信箱：terrylee830@gmail.com

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This policy explains how the Ferrokin iOS app handles data. Ferrokin does not require an account. We do not collect or receive your personal data through the app. Audio analysis, appearance settings and pet state are processed on your device.

Microphone

The app uses the microphone only when you tap "Microphone" and grant access. Sound is analysed on your device in real time for loudness and rhythm to make the visuals move with the music. The app does not record or save microphone audio, or upload or share it.

You can revoke Ferrokin's microphone access at any time in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone.

Imported audio files

Audio files you choose to import are played and analysed on your device only. Ferrokin does not upload them.

Settings and pet state

Appearance, lights, saved presets and settings such as the pet's energy are stored on your device only and are not sent to us. Deleting the app removes the app's locally stored data from the device.

In-app purchases

Payments for Ferrokin PRO are processed by Apple's App Store. We do not receive your credit card or other payment method details. For information about Apple's handling of purchase-related data, see Apple's Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

Ferrokin has no advertising, no user analytics tools, no tracking across apps or websites, and no third-party SDKs that collect personal data.

Children's privacy

We do not collect personal data from any users through the Ferrokin app, including children.

Support contact

If you choose to contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you provide, and use them only to respond to and handle your enquiry. Please do not send passwords, payment details or other unnecessary sensitive information.
