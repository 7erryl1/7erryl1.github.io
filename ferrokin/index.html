常見問題

麥克風沒有反應，怎麼辦？
在 App 內點「麥克風」並允許存取。若先前關閉了權限，請到 iPhone「設定 › 隱私權與安全性 › 麥克風」開啟 Ferrokin。
畫面和音樂節拍對不上，怎麼辦？
播放 Demo 或音檔時，可以用「音畫同步」滑桿微調畫面延遲。
換手機或重裝 App 後，怎麼找回 Ferrokin PRO？
使用原本購買的 Apple 帳號登入 App Store，打開 Ferrokin 的 PRO 解鎖區，點「恢復購買」。

Common questions

Why isn't the microphone responding?
Tap Microphone in the app and grant access. If access was turned off, go to iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone and enable Ferrokin.
What if the visuals are out of step with the music?
While playing the demo or an audio file, use the audio sync slider to adjust the picture delay.
How do I get Ferrokin PRO back on a new iPhone or after reinstalling?
Sign in to the App Store with the Apple Account you used to buy PRO. Open the PRO unlock section in Ferrokin and tap Restore purchase.
